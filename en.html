We choose something we enjoy doing and talk about what’s on your plate. A beautiful trail in Värmland is a good start. So is a bench and a flask of coffee. What drains you? What would you like to build? What can a machine handle, and where do you need your own judgement?

ONE OFFER. MANY WAYS TO EXPERIENCE IT.

Nature sets the scene.
Curiosity leads the way.

Four hours with me, Anna. We follow the season and your interests, making room for nature and conversation. You don’t need to know AI or arrive with a problem ready to solve.

01 / TRAILS & BIRDLIFE

Look up.

A quiet trail, a viewpoint or migrating birds gathering in spring and autumn. We stop when something catches our attention and give the conversation time.

02 / WATER & SHORE

A different perspective.

We glide along in a kayak or pause by the shore with a flask of coffee. Thoughts can wander on the water; we try the tools during a break on land.

03 / FOREST & FINDS

Room in your basket.

We look for mushrooms, read the landscape and talk about what’s on your mind. Bring two baskets if you like: one for golden chanterelles, one for winter chanterelles. The forest decides the harvest.

04 / WINTER & STILLNESS

Time to pause.

A winter walk, a warm drink and perhaps ice fishing when the ice and conditions allow. If you want to take your catch home, I help you prepare it for the kitchen.

A few of my own moments in Värmland. We’ll find yours.

IT STARTED ON AN AUTUMN DAY IN THE FOREST

A little mushroom website.
Room to grow.

I wanted to bring together what I love most: being outdoors, thinking freely and helping others understand what technology can do. That’s how the Adventure began.

That’s why the link still says “autumn in the forest” in Swedish. It stays as a little trace of the beginning. The ideas can keep growing all year round.

This website is also my living workshop. We experiment with code, build on it and show what’s possible. Prices from one shared spreadsheet are one example. Have an idea of your own? Bring it along.

Here’s to doing things your own way — and not giving up ;)
